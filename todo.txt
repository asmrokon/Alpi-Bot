**Prefix Commands**
- **`uml`**: Update a user's manga limit (admin-only; args: `dc_id`, `limit`).  
- **`rc`**: Reload a cog/extension (arg: `cog`).  
- **`lc`**: Load a cog/extension (arg: `cog`).  
- **`uc`**: Unload a cog/extension (arg: `cog`).  
- Source: admin.py

**App/Slash Commands (manga)**
- **`/manga search`**: Search Comick.io (args: `title`, optional `status`, `demographic`, `content_rating`, `limit`).  
- **`/manga trending`**: View trending manga (args: `day`, `comic_types`, `mature_content`).  
- **`/manga add`**: Add a manga to your list (arg: `link`).  
- **`/manga list`**: Show your manga list (arg: `mode` — `Compact` or `Detailed`).  
- **`/manga remove`**: Remove manga from your list (args: `title` with autocomplete OR `mode` — `Selection menu` / `Remove All`).  
- Note: these are in the `manga` command group.  
- Source: manga.py

**App/Slash Commands (news)**
- **`/news`**: View latest anime news (arg: `source` — `Crunchyroll News` or `MyAnimeList News`).  
- **`/subscribe`**: Subscribe to news DMs (arg: `source`).  
- **`/unsubscribe`**: Unsubscribe from news DMs (arg: `source`).  
- Source: news.py
